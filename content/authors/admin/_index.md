## About Me

I am a **Postdoctoral Research Fellow** at the Advanced Computing and Digital Engineering Institute, part of the Key Laboratory of High-Performance Data Mining at the [Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)](https://www.siat.ac.cn/).

I work under [**Professor Qiang Qu**](https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~qiang?language=en), who is the director of the Guangdong Provincial Blockchain and Distributed IoT Security Engineering Research Center and the deputy director of the Shenzhen High-Performance Data Mining Key Laboratory.

## 📢 News and Announcements

### 🔍 Recruiting Ph.D. Students and PostDocs!
- We are looking for candidates in **Generative AI, Large Models, Web3, Blockchain, and Distributed Storage Systems**.
- Successful Ph.D. applicants can apply for the **[ANSO Scholarship](http://www.anso.org.cn/programmes/talent/scholarship/)**.
- ⏳ **Deadline:** Typically February/March each year (early contact recommended).
- 📧 **Postdoc applications are accepted year-round!** However, applicants must be **under 35 years** of age and have received their **Ph.D. within the last 3 years**, as per the China Postdoctoral Council.
