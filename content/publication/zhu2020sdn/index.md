abstract = "In this article, we present a comprehensive qualitative comparison of different SDN controllers, along with a quantitative analysis of their performance in different network scenarios. More specifically, we categorize and classify 34 controllers based on their capabilities, and present a qualitative comparison of their properties. We also discuss in-depth capabilities of benchmarking tools used for SDN controllers, along with best practices for quantitative controller evaluation. This work uses three benchmarking tools to compare nine controllers against multiple criteria. Finally, we discuss detailed research findings on the performance, benchmarking criteria, and evaluation testbeds for SDN controllers."
