Research

My research agenda centers on the design, implementation, and evaluation of trustworthy intelligent systems across decentralized and resource-constrained environments.

  • Decentralized and agentic AI: coordination, governance, and secure collaboration among autonomous AI agents.
  • Blockchain and Web3 systems: scalable, secure, and intelligent decentralized infrastructures.
  • IoT, edge, and distributed computing: computation, communication, and resource management for networked systems.
  • Trustworthy distributed intelligence: verifiable execution, privacy, security, and system-level evaluation.
