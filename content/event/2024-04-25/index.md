abstract = "In this talk, I explored the transformative potential of merging Web3 and Artificial Intelligence (AI). This integration can revolutionize the decentralized ecosystem. The presentation highlighted its core concepts and key enablers, such as blockchain technology, decentralized applications (DApps), and machine learning. The discussion covered real-world use cases where Web3 and AI converge. These technologies create intelligent, autonomous applications that enhance sectors like fintech, supply chain management, and healthcare. By integrating AI’s predictive analytics and adaptive algorithms with Web3’s decentralized and trustless framework, we unlocked new possibilities for secure and efficient digital interactions. Further, I examined the future potentials of this integration. These include enhanced data privacy, improved financial services through Decentralized Finance (DeFi), and innovative applications in the metaverse. This talk underscored the importance of collaborative efforts across industries. Such efforts are crucial to develop unified standards and address the challenges posed by this technological convergence. The session demonstrated how Web3 and AI together pave the way for a new era of decentralized innovation and economic transformation."
