# Agent Task 04: Draft Academic Outreach Emails

## Goal

Prepare careful academic outreach emails for researchers whose work is directly related to the survey. These emails should introduce the paper as a useful taxonomy/background resource. They must not pressure anyone to cite the paper.

## Required input

Use the target list produced from `03-researcher-target-list.md`.

For each recipient, the email should be lightly personalized based on:

- Recipient name
- Recent paper title
- Topic overlap
- Why the trust-to-augmentation survey may be useful

## Core paper details

Md Monjurul Karim, Sangeen Khan, Qiang Qu, Muhammad Muzammal, Kashif Sharif, and Sujit Biswas, "From trust to augmentation: A comprehensive survey on synergistic integration of decentralized and generative intelligence," *Computer Science Review*, vol. 61, article 100936, 2026. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cosrev.2026.100936>.

Project page:
<https://karimmd.github.io/paper-pages/karim2026trust/>

## Tone requirements

- Academic and respectful.
- Concise.
- No exaggerated claims.
- No direct request for citation.
- No mass-mail style.
- Mention the recipient's work only if it has been verified.

## Template 1: Individual researcher

Subject: Related survey on decentralized and generative intelligence

Dear Dr. [Surname],

I read your recent work on [specific topic/paper title], particularly its focus on [specific overlap]. I thought our recent survey in *Computer Science Review* may be relevant to this line of work.

The paper, "From trust to augmentation: A comprehensive survey on synergistic integration of decentralized and generative intelligence," introduces a trust-to-augmentation framework that organizes blockchain, Web3, DeAI, GenAI, and LLMs into four interdependent layers: trust-based execution, privacy-preserving interoperable middleware, collaborative learning mesh, and generative augmentation.

Project page: https://karimmd.github.io/paper-pages/karim2026trust/
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cosrev.2026.100936

I hope it is useful as a taxonomy or background reference for your related research.

Best regards,
Md Monjurul Karim

## Personalization rules

For each email, fill:

- `[Surname]`
- `[specific topic/paper title]`
- `[specific overlap]`

Do not personalize based on unverified assumptions.

## Review checklist

Before any email is sent manually:

- Recipient's name is correct.
- Affiliation is correct.
- The referenced paper actually belongs to the recipient.
- The topic overlap is real.
- The email does not ask directly for a citation.
- The email includes DOI and project page.
- Karim approves the final recipient list and message.

## Deliverable

Produce:

1. A generic final template.
2. A personalized draft for each high-priority researcher.
3. A table mapping each draft to recipient, source paper, evidence URL, and email status.
