# karim2026trust Citation-Growth Action Plan

This folder contains separate, agent-ready instruction files for increasing the visibility and citation potential of the paper:

**Paper:** Md Monjurul Karim, Sangeen Khan, Qiang Qu, Muhammad Muzammal, Kashif Sharif, and Sujit Biswas, "From trust to augmentation: A comprehensive survey on synergistic integration of decentralized and generative intelligence," *Computer Science Review*, vol. 61, article 100936, 2026. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cosrev.2026.100936>.

**Project page:** <https://karimmd.github.io/paper-pages/karim2026trust/>

## Purpose

The paper has high citation potential because it is a survey/taxonomy paper that connects blockchain, Web3, DeAI, GenAI, and LLMs through a trust-to-augmentation framework. The objective is not to ask people to cite the work directly. The objective is to make the paper easy to discover, easy to understand, and easy to use as a background/taxonomy reference in related research.

## Files and suggested agent assignment

1. `01-website-citation-landing-page.md`
   - Agent task: improve the project page so researchers can quickly cite and reuse the paper.

2. `02-academic-indexing-and-profiles.md`
   - Agent task: check academic indexing, metadata consistency, and author/profile visibility.

3. `03-researcher-target-list.md`
   - Agent task: collect candidate researchers and papers where this survey is topically relevant.

4. `04-outreach-email-drafts.md`
   - Agent task: prepare careful, non-spam academic emails and message templates.

5. `05-social-media-and-news-posts.md`
   - Agent task: prepare LinkedIn, X/Twitter, ResearchGate, and institutional-news content.

6. `06-citation-monitoring.md`
   - Agent task: set up a monthly monitoring process for citations and visibility.

## Important constraints for all agents

- Do not fabricate researcher names, affiliations, emails, citation counts, or paper relevance.
- Do not scrape aggressively or violate website terms of service.
- Do not send emails automatically unless Karim explicitly approves the final list and message.
- Do not ask researchers to cite the paper directly. The acceptable framing is: "this survey may be useful as a taxonomy/background reference for your related work."
- Keep the tone academic, polite, and concise.
- Prefer verifiable sources: Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Crossref, Scopus/Web of Science if available, DBLP, ORCID, institutional pages, publisher pages, and author homepages.
- For email collection, use only publicly listed professional emails. If an email is unavailable, record `Not found` and provide the source checked.

## Recommended execution order

1. Improve the project page and citation materials.
2. Check indexing/profile consistency.
3. Build the target researcher/paper list.
4. Draft outreach and social posts.
5. Launch approved outreach manually.
6. Monitor citations monthly.
